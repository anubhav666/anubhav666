I'm Anubhav — an electronics nerd turned embedded systems engineer who loves tinkering with hardware, writing firmware, and making things just work.

💡 I dive deep into embedded C/C++, RTOS, and microcontrollers like ESP32, STM32, and Raspberry Pi. Whether it’s building custom serial tools, automating test setups, or playing around with Modbus and PLCs, I’m always up for a challenge.

🛠️ Tools I’m comfy with:
VxWorks, KiCAD, AutoCAD, logic analyzers, oscilloscopes, MQTT, UART, RS-485, and the usual suspects like Git, Linux, and even a bit of AngularJS & .NET.

I’m especially excited about systems that bridge the physical and digital world — think robotics, IoT, and anything that feels like a step toward Industry 4.0.

Thanks for stopping by — feel free to check out my projects or just say hi!
